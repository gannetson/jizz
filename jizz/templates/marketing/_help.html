How can I help?

Birdr is a small project. If you use it, two things help more than anything else: honest feedback, and a few minutes checking that quiz photos show the right bird.

Send feedback

Found a bug, a missing species, or something that worked well? There is a feedback box on the app home screen, or write to info@birdr.pro.
